# Readium

## Overview

A publishing and social reading platform where users can create, edit, publish, and manage their own stories while discovering and reading content from other writers. Users can interact with stories through likes, bookmarks, and comments, as well as report inappropriate content. The platform also provides users with tools to manage their content and account, while administrators can moderate users, stories, comments, and reports to maintain the quality and integrity of the platform.
